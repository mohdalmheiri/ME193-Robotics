# Robustness test: the real motor's specs in FakeRobot

- Policy: `train/logs/rsl_rl/walk_straight_v2/2026-11-13_18-36-30_v2/model_799.pt`. 100 random lopsided FakeRobots (seeds 0-99, the same robots in every row), 20 s each, through `robot/run_policy.py` (generated by `robot/check_spec_motor.py`).
- Spec motor (Julian, 2026-11-14, source to be confirmed): no-load 322-346 rpm, stall 0.148-0.173 N m at 4.2 V, each side drawn from these ranges; body 80 g (training: 100 g x 0.6-1.6). Training motor: 0.10 N m limit (x0.8-1.2), any speed.
- "Hub holds speed": training's speed controller (0.05 N m per rad/s of error), limited by the DC motor's torque at its current speed. "No speed control (PWM)": percent = voltage, torque = stall x (percent/100 - speed/no-load speed). Which one the LEGO hub does is unknown until Monday's calibration (measure_rpm.py on the stand vs on the table).
- The policy's action becomes an rpm inside the trained range FIRST (full action = POLICY_TOP_RPM), then a percent from the rpm table (placeholder: 334 rpm at 100 %).
- Values: mean / median / worst 5 % of the absolute value at the end; forward: mean; max tilt: median and worst of each robot's largest body tilt (flip = 70 deg).
